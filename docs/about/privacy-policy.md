# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: March 18, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to TaughtCode Server. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our backend services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1. Personal Information
We may collect personal information that you provide to us, such as:
- **Account Information**: Email address, display name, occupation, and bio.
- **Authentication Data**: Credentials and authentication tokens (e.g., Firebase Auth data).
- **Profile Assets**: Profile pictures, cover photos, and gallery assets.

### 2.2. Technical Information
When you access our API, we automatically collect certain information:
- **Log Data**: IP address, browser type, operating system, and access times.
- **Usage Data**: Details of how you use our services, including request patterns and API interactions.
- **Device Information**: Information about the device used to access the services.

### 2.3. AI Interaction Data
As an AI-orchestrated server, we collect data related to your interactions with AI models:
- **Prompts and Queries**: Text or data you submit for AI processing.
- **Generated Content**: The output generated by AI models in response to your requests.
- **Feedback**: Any corrections or feedback provided to improve AI accuracy.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes, including:
- **Providing and Managing Services**: To facilitate account creation, authentication, and user profile management.
- **AI Orchestration**: To process prompts and generate content using integrated AI providers (e.g., Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic).
- **Service Improvement**: To analyze usage trends and improve the functionality and performance of our backend.
- **Communication**: To send administrative information, such as updates to our terms or policies.
- **Security**: To monitor for and prevent fraudulent activity and ensure the safety of our services.

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information in the following situations:
- **AI Service Providers**: We share necessary data (like prompts) with AI providers (e.g., Google, OpenAI) to fulfill your requests for AI-generated content. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies.
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: Data is stored and processed using cloud service providers such as Firebase (Google Cloud).
- **Third-Party Integrations**: If you choose to connect third-party services (e.g., GitHub, LinkedIn), we share data required for those integrations as authorized by you.
- **Legal Requirements**: We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, please remember that no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as:
- **Access**: The right to request copies of your personal data.
- **Correction**: The right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
- **Deletion**: The right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
- **Object to Processing**: The right to object to our processing of your personal data.

## 7. AI Data Retention and Usage

Data submitted to AI models is handled according to the specific provider's data processing agreements. We do not use your private data to train foundation models unless explicitly stated or authorized.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Effective Date" and will be effective as soon as it is accessible.
